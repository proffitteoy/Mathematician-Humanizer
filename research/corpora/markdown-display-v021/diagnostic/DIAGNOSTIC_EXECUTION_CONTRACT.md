## Separate shared budget

The cumulative maximum for the natural execution and independent readback is 60 seconds. To make accounting conservative and unambiguous, reserve at most 30 seconds for the entire executor, including its binding checks and terminal work, and at most 30 seconds for the later bounded independent readback. Unused execution time is not silently borrowed to enlarge readback. Waiting between the two stages is not executing either stage.

The executor's monotonic timer begins on CLI entry. Its deadline includes preflight binding checks and terminal writes; a remaining-time kernel-backed alarm and CPU limit are applied before source access. The readback must use its own fixed 30-second guard and account for default validator replay as real computation. Serial execution/readback is required; a separate reader may not race the executor.

The maximum for the combined package and audit derivatives is 4 MiB. The declared charged roots are:

- the new diagnostic-runner package
- the corrected v0.2.1 implementation package
- the new v0.2.1 diagnostic-review package
- the existing v0.2 correction-review package, conservatively charged in full

The existing acquisition raw corpus and unchanged v0.1 packages are inputs, not new derivatives. They receive no new writes. At least 64 KiB of terminal-evidence capacity is reserved inside the 4 MiB total; ordinary writes cannot consume it. Terminal writes may use it but remain capped at 4 MiB. All partial files, logs, approvals, manifests, audit files and failed outputs in the charged roots count. The proposal fixes these roots; changing them is not a permitted workaround.

Address space is limited to 512 MiB and observed peak RSS is recorded. At most two CPU affinity slots and numerical threads are allowed; GPU is disabled. Seccomp denies network and child creation/execution, and an import audit denies NLP/model packages. No package installation or download occurs.

The marker is new and version-specific: `v021-diagnostic.ONE_TIME_STARTED.private.json`. It is exclusive and remains consumed after every failure. The v0.1 marker is never touched or reused.

Source symlinks, changed lengths and changed hashes fail closed. Failure identity stays private. No automatic retry, alternate directory, resume, replacement, marker deletion or cap increase is authorized.

The projection envelope preserves the exact v0.2.1 result hash and retains all characters' raw maps, multiple source spans, syntax-elision proofs, continuity edges, total partitions, content boundaries and conservative table scopes. Added diagnostic metadata is outside the hashed projection. The profile's internal validation is not falsely described as an independent default-replay readback.

Each private gzip output and its directory are fsynced before the projection is counted as completed. A terminal receipt and safe aggregate are bound by an exclusive terminal seal. Durable success requires a successful executor exit, the verified seal, verified referenced files, and no failure artifact. If terminal persistence or deadline enforcement fails, no success may be reported; an emergency failure artifact is attempted within the reserved cap. A partial or missing terminal artifact is failure evidence, never permission to rerun.

Root must arrange a bounded independent readback after a sealed execution. It may inspect only these exact three source identities and sealed outputs. It must verify the manifest/GO/code/envelope/output hashes, raw maps, full partitions, syntax-only edge proofs and table/container scopes, plus public `validate_projection` with its default replay enabled. Default replay re-derives from already supplied bytes and counts against the readback's 30 seconds. It is not a new source-file read, but it is not free computation.

A one-time readback marker, private exposure/accounting ledger and final readback receipt must be used in the declared audit root. The readback's own code/budget authority must be bound before it starts; this executor does not automatically launch it. If it cannot fit the remaining fixed sub-budget, stop and report the blocker, without cap borrowing or replaying this executor.
