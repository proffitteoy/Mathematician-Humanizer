# Additional corpus admission check

Checked 2026-10-01. No PKU or LCMC data downloaded, and no access agreement accepted. The separately documented small WikiConv pilot does not change these decisions.

## PKU Modern Chinese multilevel corpus: restricted, not admitted

Primary source: https://opendata.pku.edu.cn/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.18170/DVN/SEYRX5

The repository describes 1998 People's Daily annotated material. Its Chinese description counts 52 million characters for basic annotation; the English metadata says words, so retain this unit discrepancy rather than propagating a token count. Available restricted archives are samples, not necessarily that whole corpus.

Despite a general CC0 badge, file-specific access terms limit use to personal noncommercial academic research and prohibit redistribution of all or part of downloaded text. Three files are restricted. The page states users may not request file access. Therefore this is a provenance reference and possible future separately authorized resource, not currently admitted reusable training data. Do not upload its contents to the GitHub project.

## LCMC: official terms verified, agreement not accepted

Catalogue URL: https://llds.phon.ox.ac.uk/llds/xmlui/handle/20.500.14106/2474?show=full

The initial catalogue page returned an access-denied response. A subsequent read of the [official Lancaster EULA](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/fass/projects/corpus/LCMC/lcmc/LCMCorder.asp) verified a separate licence: research use is permitted under its conditions and nonprofit research is free, but sharing processed corpus material is restricted to the licensee/research group; text reuse and extracts have additional restrictions (§§2–3). These specific terms must not be replaced by an earlier unverified catalogue CC claim. No acceptance form was submitted and no corpus was downloaded. LCMC remains quarantined; any future agreement/access step requires its own authorization. No mirror was used to evade the initial restriction.
